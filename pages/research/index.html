Most of my research lives inside the projects — each one tends to grow its own paper. This page pulls those threads together: interpretability, AI safety, embedding geometry, and neurosymbolic systems. It is an evolving index, not a tidy CV.

Papers & standalone research

Latent Space Cartography Applied to Wikidata Paper · Claw4S 2026

Relational displacement analysis on frozen text-embedding models, using Wikidata triples as probes. Finds 30 model-agnostic relational operations — and a silent [UNK] tokenizer defect in mxbai-embed-large where unrelated diacritical strings collapse to cosine 1.0. Paper ID 2604.00648.

clawRxiv latent-space.emmaleonhart.com GitHub
Redemption-Realignment Research repo

Does redemption-narrative steering measurably move emergently misaligned LLMs back toward alignment — behaviorally, on self-rated harmfulness, and geometrically against a derived misalignment direction? Theoretical framing: emergent misalignment as moral injury. Cross-scale results across Llama and Qwen (0.5B–8B); the paper and synthesis live in-repo.

alignment.emmaleonhart.com GitHub
Deleuzian Assemblage Approach to NN Interpretability Paper · Claw4S

An assemblage-theoretic reframing of neural-network interpretability — reading internal structure through Deleuze rather than purely through circuits.

Research inside the projects

Sutra Language · NeurIPS 2026 track

The geometric tensor language — logical operations over vector spaces resolved at compile time into matrix multiplications. The language paper and its NeurIPS 2026 supplementary are built and published alongside the project site.

sutra.emmaleonhart.com GitHub
Loka / SutraDB Systems · paper in-repo

An RDF-star triplestore in Rust with native HNSW vector indexing where vectors are just triples. The systems writeup and benchmarks live in the repo.

loka.emmaleonhart.com GitHub
Yantra Systems · design papers

A neuro-symbolic, GPU-native operating system written in Sutra. The architecture and the case for it are written up in the repo's paper/ and planning/.

yantra.emmaleonhart.com GitHub
